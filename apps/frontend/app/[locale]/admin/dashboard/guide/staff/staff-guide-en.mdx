# Staff User Guide

## Scope and Responsibilities

The Staff role focuses on assisting their respective agency in addressing questions submitted through AskMyGov. Below are the key responsibilities of a Staff member:

- Viewing Agency-Specific Questions
  - Staff members can view all questions assigned to their agency, ensuring they are aware of public inquiries relevant to their department.
  - Access is limited strictly to questions directed to their agency.
- Answering Agency-Assigned Questions
  - Staff are responsible for providing accurate and timely responses to questions assigned to their agency.
  - Responses should adhere to the platform’s standards for clarity, professionalism, and relevance.
- Collaborating with Agency Teams
  - Work with other staff members or supervisors within the agency to gather information needed to address complex queries.
  - Escalate questions to higher-level officials if additional approval or expertise is required.
- Maintaining Answer Quality
  - Ensure all answers are well-researched, factually accurate, and aligned with the agency’s guidelines and policies.
  - Edit or update responses as necessary to reflect the latest information or directives from the agency.

## Features and Functionalities

### Managing Questions

1. Click on a question to answer it.
   ![Click a question to answer](/guide/staff/click-question-to-answer.png)

2. To edit an answer, hover over the question and click **Edit**.
   ![Edit an answer](/guide/staff/edit-answer-button.png)

3. A dialog will pop up for you to answer the question. You can also upload files and assign topics to the question.

- Click **Save as draft** to save the question without publishing it. You may publish the question and answer later.
- Click **Publish now** to save and publish the question.
  ![Answer question button](/guide/staff/question-answer-form.png)

4. Questions can be filtered by clicking on one of the tabs. You can also search for a question with the search bar.
   ![Filtering questions](/guide/staff/filter-questions.png)
