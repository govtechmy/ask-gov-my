# Super Admin User Guide

## Scope and Responsibilities

The Super Admin role is critical in ensuring the smooth operation and quality of AskMyGov. Below are the key responsibilities of a Super Admin:

- Reviewing Publicly Submitted Questions
  - Super Admins are responsible for reviewing all questions submitted by the public to ensure they meet the platform’s standards and guidelines.
  - Questions should be relevant, clear, and appropriate for agency responses.
- Filtering Out Bad or Low-Quality Questions
  - Identify and remove questions that are spam, irrelevant, offensive, or incoherent.
- Assigning Questions to Agencies
  - Match valid questions to the appropriate government agency for resolution.
  - Ensure each question is routed to the correct agency based on its subject matter.
- Answering Questions Directly
  - For common or straightforward queries, the Super Admin can respond directly without agency involvement.
  - Use this option sparingly and only for questions that fall within the scope of general knowledge or platform guidelines.

## Features and Functionalities

### Managing Users

1. Click on the **New user** button to create a new user. When a user is created, they can sign-in with their email.
   ![New user button](/guide/super-admin/user-add-button.png)

2. To edit a user, click the options menu icon, then click on **Edit user**.
   ![Edit user button](/guide/super-admin/user-edit-button.png)

3. Fill in the form and click **Save**.
   ![User form](/guide/super-admin/user-form.png)

You can assign a role to the user:

- **Super Admin**: Have permissions to view and manage all questions, agencies and users.
- **Staff**: Can only view and manage questions in their agency.

### Managing Agencies

1. Click the **New agency** button to add new agencies.
   ![How to add a new agency](/guide/super-admin/add-agency.png)

2. To edit an agency, hover over the agency and click the edit icon.
   ![How to edit an agency](/guide/super-admin/edit-agency.png)

3. Fill in the form and click **Save**.
   ![Saving agency details](/guide/super-admin/agency-form-save.png)

### Managing Questions

1.  To mark a question as spam, hover over the question and click on the options menu icon. Then, click **Mark as Spam**.
    ![Mark a question as spam](/guide/super-admin/mark-question-as-spam.png)

2.  Questions can be filtered by clicking on one of the tabs. You can also filter by agency or search for a question.
    ![Filtering questions](/guide/super-admin/filter-questions.png)

3.  To assign questions to an agency, click on the agency dropdown and select an agency.
    ![Question agency dropdown](/guide/super-admin/question-agency-dropdown.png)
    ![Assign an agency to a question](/guide/super-admin/question-assign-agency.png)

### Answering Questions Directly

1. Before you can answer a question, it must be assigned to an agency first.
2. After assigning an agency, click on the options menu icon. Then, click **Answer question**.
   ![Answer question button](/guide/super-admin/answer-question-button.png)
3. A dialog will pop up for you to answer the question. You can also upload files and assign topics to the question.

- Click **Save as draft** to save the question without publishing it. You may publish the question and answer later.
- Click **Publish now** to save and publish the question.
  ![Answer question button](/guide/super-admin/answer-question-form.png)
